## Part F: Standard Form and Converting (10 problems)

The **standard form** is:

$$y = ax^2 + bx + c$$

To find the vertex from standard form:

$$x_{\text{vertex}} = \frac{-b}{2a}$$

Then plug this $x$ back in to find $y_{\text{vertex}}$.

## Part G: Roots and the Discriminant — Graphical View (10 problems)

The **x-intercepts** (roots) of a parabola are where $y = 0$.

The **discriminant** $b^2 - 4ac$ tells us how many roots to expect:

| Discriminant | Roots | Graph |
|--------------|-------|-------|
| $b^2 - 4ac > 0$ | Two distinct real roots | Crosses x-axis twice |
| $b^2 - 4ac = 0$ | One repeated root | Touches x-axis once (vertex on x-axis) |
| $b^2 - 4ac < 0$ | No real roots | Never crosses x-axis |
