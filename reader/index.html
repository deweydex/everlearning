Read Aloud

Private, on-device AI speech. Paste English text, choose a KittenTTS model and voice, and listen or download the result. No paid speech API required.

1. Voice settings

The first model load downloads weights from Hugging Face and runtime assets from a CDN. They may be cached by your browser. Changing model size requires loading a different model.

2. Your text

Ready. Choose Generate & play to load the model.

For long documents, the app splits text into smaller sections and combines the audio into one WAV. Generating an entire long document can require considerable time and memory on mobile devices.

Speech is synthesized on your device. The KittenTTS developer-preview SDK is used with analytics disabled. Your text is not sent to a speech API. This page loads an external SDK script and models; an initial internet connection is required.
