# Inventory

This file lists everything in everlearning at the level of a series or a collection, not single files. The destination column is a proposal. A question mark means the decision is still open.

Destinations use five words. **dewlab** means the material becomes an interactive tutorial there. **site** means it gets a write-up on jsaaron.com that links back here or to its live home. **print** means it is offered as a printable download. **keep** means it stays here as source material with no public face. **archive** means it is superseded.
