## Part A: Coordinate Basics (10 problems)

**1.** Plot the following points on a coordinate plane and label each with its coordinates:
- A = (3, 4)
- B = (-2, 5)
- C = (-4, -3)
- D = (5, -2)
- E = (0, 3)
- F = (-3, 0)

**2.** In which quadrant (I, II, III, IV) or on which axis does each point lie?
- (a) (7, -2)
- (b) (-3, -8)
- (c) (0, 5)
- (d) (-4, 6)
- (e) (2, 0)

**3.** Find the distance between each pair of points:
- (a) (0, 0) and (3, 4)
- (b) (1, 2) and (4, 6)
- (c) (-2, 3) and (4, -1)
- (d) (-5, -2) and (3, 4)

**4.** Find the midpoint of the segment connecting:
- (a) (2, 8) and (6, 2)
- (b) (-3, 4) and (5, -2)
- (c) (0, 7) and (-4, -1)

**5.** A point starts at (2, 3) and moves 4 units right and 5 units down. What are its new coordinates?

**6.** The midpoint of segment PQ is (3, 1). If P = (7, 4), what are the coordinates of Q?

## Part B: Vectors (12 problems)

Vectors are written in bold: **v**, **w**, **u**. They have both magnitude (length) and direction.

**7.** Given **v** = (3, 4) and **w** = (1, -2), calculate:
- (a) **v** + **w**
- (b) **v** - **w**
- (c) **w** - **v**
- (d) 2**v**
- (e) -3**w**
- (f) 2**v** + 3**w**

**8.** Find the magnitude (length) of each vector. Recall: |**v**| = √(v₁² + v₂²)
- (a) **v** = (3, 4)
- (b) **w** = (5, 12)
- (c) **u** = (-2, 2)
- (d) **p** = (1, 1)

**9.** A unit vector has magnitude 1. Find the unit vector in the same direction as:
- (a) **v** = (3, 4)
- (b) **w** = (0, 5)

*(Hint: divide each component by the magnitude)*

**10.** Vector **a** = (4, 3) and vector **b** = (-3, 4).
- (a) Calculate **a** + **b**
- (b) Calculate |**a**| and |**b**|
- (c) Calculate |**a** + **b**|
- (d) Is |**a** + **b**| equal to |**a**| + |**b**|? Why or why not?

**11.** A drone is at position (2, 5). It moves by vector **v** = (3, -2), then by vector **w** = (-1, 4). What is its final position?

**12.** The zero vector is **0** = (0, 0). For any vector **v**:
- (a) What is **v** + **0**?
- (b) What is **v** + (-**v**)?
- (c) What vector **w** satisfies **v** + **w** = **0**?

**13.** Given points P = (1, 2) and Q = (4, 6), find the vector **PQ** that goes from P to Q.

*(Hint: **PQ** = Q - P)*

**14.** If **v** = (2, 5) and **w** = (k, 15) are parallel (same direction), what is k?
